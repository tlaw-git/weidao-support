Frequently asked questions 常见问题

How do I add a recipe by voice?

Tap the red microphone in the middle of the tab bar, then tap the large record button and say the recipe the way you would tell it to family: ingredients, amounts and steps. Tap again to stop. Wèidào transcribes the recording, translates it and shows you an editable draft with the ingredients and steps in both English and Chinese. Check it over, then save.

Which languages can I speak?

English, Mandarin and Cantonese. You can mix languages in one recording, and Cantonese kitchen terms are kept as spoken.

Can I switch between Simplified and Traditional Chinese?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Simplified 简 or Traditional 繁 under Chinese Script. Every recipe updates immediately. Recipes are stored in Simplified and converted for display, so switching never changes what you saved.

Can I type a recipe instead of recording it?

Yes. On the Add screen, tap Enter manually. You can fill in either language or both; if you leave one side blank the other is used for both.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start adding recipes.

Where are my recipes stored?

On your device. Wèidào keeps your recipes in a local database on your phone, not on our servers. If you delete the app, the recipes on that device are deleted with it.

The microphone button says access is needed

Wèidào needs microphone permission to record. On iPhone, open Settings, scroll to Wèidào and turn on Microphone. On Android, open Settings, then Apps, then Wèidào, then Permissions. You can always add recipes manually without the microphone.

Voice capture says it is unavailable

Voice capture needs an internet connection to transcribe and translate. Check your connection and try again. Manual entry works offline.

How do I delete my data?

Because recipes live only on your device, deleting the app removes them. To have any support correspondence removed, email us at the address above.

Privacy 隐私

Wèidào collects no personal information and has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

When you record a recipe, the audio is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, where it is transcribed and translated and the structured recipe is returned to your phone. The audio is used only for that purpose. Your saved recipes are stored on your device.

If you email support, we keep your message only for as long as needed to help you.
