Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This policy covers the 味道 Wèidào app for iOS and Android (Google Play: com.f22clarity.weidao), published by F22 Clarity.

In this policy, “we” means F22 Clarity, the developer of 味道 Wèidào (“Wèidào” or “the app”). Wèidào is built to keep family recipes, not to collect information about you. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell your information or share it for marketing, and nothing you record or write is used to train our own models.

What stays on your device 保存在手机上

Your recipes live in a database on your phone: titles, ingredients, steps in English and Chinese, categories, timings and bookmarks. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot see them. Your language and script preferences are stored on the phone too. Deleting a recipe in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

Voice capture 语音录入

Only when you record a recipe by voice, two things leave your device:

The audio recording

It is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted by Supabase), which forwards it to OpenAI to be turned into text. We do not keep the audio.

The transcribed text

It is sent to Anthropic’s Claude, which organises it into ingredients and steps and writes it in both English and Chinese. The recipe comes back to your phone and is saved there. We do not keep a copy.

These providers process the content to complete the request and may keep it briefly for abuse monitoring under their own policies: the OpenAI privacy policy and the Anthropic privacy policy.

The microphone is used only while you are recording, which you start and stop yourself. Recordings stop at 10 minutes. On iPhone, leaving the app also ends the recording. On Android, if you lock your phone or switch apps mid-recording, it keeps recording, with a notification showing, until you tap stop or it reaches 10 minutes. It is never used at any other time.

Your account 账户

Browsing and adding recipes by hand need no account. To use voice capture you sign in with your email address; we email you a one-time code, so there is no password. When you first sign in we ask for your name. We store your email address, your name, whether you asked to receive news about Wèidào, and when you signed up, on our server.

We use your email to sign you in and to count how many of your free voice recipes you have used. We only send you news about Wèidào if you switched that option on, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Subscriptions 订阅

Your first three voice recipes are free. After that, voice capture needs a monthly or yearly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or billing details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active (RevenueCat privacy policy). RevenueCat receives your account identifier, your Wèidào purchase history, and your email address and name so we can recognise subscribers.

Sharing a recipe 分享食谱

Subscribers can share a recipe by email. Wèidào writes the email on your phone and hands it to your own mail app, where you choose who to send it to. The recipe and the address never pass through our server.

Deleting your account 删除账户

Delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account), or without the app on our account deletion page. This removes your email address, name, subscription status and usage records from our server, and your customer record at RevenueCat.

To stop the free voice recipes being reset by deleting and signing up again, we keep a one-way hash of your email address (not the address itself) and the number of free recipes it has used. Deleting your account does not touch the recipes on your phone, and it does not cancel a subscription: cancel that in your App Store or Google Play account.

Permissions 权限

The app asks for microphone access only for voice capture, the first time you tap record. You can decline and still add recipes by hand. It does not ask for contacts, photos, location or advertising identifiers.

Children 儿童

Wèidào is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes 变更

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
