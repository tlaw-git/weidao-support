What is deleted 删除的内容

Straight away: your email address, name and news preference; your subscription status and usage records on our server; and your customer record at RevenueCat, the service that checks subscriptions for us.

What is kept 保留的内容

A one-way hash of your email address (not the address itself) and how many of your 3 free voice recipes were used. This stops the free recipes being reset by deleting and signing up again, and it can’t be turned back into your email address.

Your recipes 您的食谱

Recipes are stored only on your phone, never on our server, so deleting your account doesn’t touch them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Subscriptions 订阅

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions).
